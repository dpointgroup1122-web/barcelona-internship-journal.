01 THE EXPERIENCE

More than just
an internship.

Coming to Barcelona for an international internship has been one of those experiences that changes your routine completely — in a good way.

Living in a new city means learning every day, even outside work. I have met people from different countries, discovered new places and become part of an international environment. Barcelona makes that easy: there is always somewhere to go, something to see or someone new to meet.

The experience has also pushed me to be more independent. From adapting to a different working environment to building a new network from scratch, the small challenges have become some of the most valuable parts of the journey.

02 WORK & GROWTH

Learning by doing.

My internship at Dpoint has given me a look at how a Barcelona-based company works across digital services, marketing, events and online projects.

I have been working through practical tasks involving online communication, social media tools and web content. I also use part of my time to strengthen technical foundations such as Python and the tools I will need for DevOps-related work.

What I value most is the international side of the experience. Working abroad puts you in situations where you have to communicate, adapt and solve things independently — skills that are difficult to learn from a classroom alone.

03 JOURNAL NOTES

Small notes from
the experience.

Not every part of an internship fits into a formal report. These are a few things I have noticed along the way.

BARCELONA · LIFE ABROAD

Learning a city while learning a job

Moving abroad means that the learning continues after work. New transport, new routines, new people and a different pace of life all become part of the experience. After the first days, Barcelona slowly starts to feel less unfamiliar.

WORK · DEVELOPMENT

Building skills outside the task list

Alongside the internship assignments, I have been keeping time for technical practice. Revisiting Python and preparing for DevOps topics helps me connect the general tasks with the direction I want to develop professionally.

PEOPLE · NETWORKING

The international side matters

One of the strongest parts of being abroad is meeting people with completely different backgrounds. Even short conversations can turn into useful connections, recommendations or simply a different way of looking at something.

DPOINT SPOTLIGHT

Events can happen almost anywhere.

One of the more visual sides of Dpoint's work is its event and audiovisual activity. Inflatable screens are a simple example: a temporary space can become an outdoor cinema, presentation area or event setting without a permanent installation.

For me, it was interesting to see how a physical product can also depend on good online communication, visual content and promotion to reach the right audience.

04 LOOKING FORWARD

Still learning,
still exploring.

The experience is still in progress, and that is what makes the journal useful.

There are still technical tasks to work toward, more of Barcelona to experience and more opportunities to learn from the people around me. I want to keep the blog simple and update it with the parts of the internship that are actually worth remembering.

For students considering an internship abroad, the biggest value is not one individual task. It is the combination of work, independence, new connections and learning how to adapt somewhere new.
